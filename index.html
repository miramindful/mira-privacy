Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

MIRA is a personal spending reflection app from Lumine Insights, designed to help users understand their relationship with spending.

Information MIRA Stores

MIRA allows you to record information such as:

  • Purchases and purchase amounts
  • Whether a purchase was planned
  • Emotions associated with a purchase
  • Personal reflections
  • Whether you regret a purchase
  • Mindful Moments and reflections

This information is stored locally on your device. MIRA does not require an account or login and does not transmit this information to a server operated by the developer.

Purchases

MIRA+ is an optional one-time purchase processed through Apple's App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android. MIRA does not receive or store your payment card details, such as your credit card number.

Data Collection

MIRA does not collect, sell, or share your personal spending information with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

MIRA does not use advertising or third-party analytics to track users.

Data Deletion

Because your spending information is stored locally on your device, you can remove the information by deleting the MIRA app from your device.

Children's Privacy

MIRA is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when MIRA's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be made available at this URL.
